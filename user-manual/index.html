🚀 1. Getting Started

Welcome! This section shows you how to log in, create an account, and navigate the system.

🔐 How to Log In

1

Open the System

Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) and go to the system address provided by your administrator.

2

Enter Your Email ✉️

Type your email address in the Email field.

3

Enter Your Password 🔑

Type your password. 👆 Click the eye icon 👁️ to show/hide your password.

4

👆 Click Sign In

You will be taken to your Dashboard. If you see an error, check your email and password.

💡
First time? Ask your administrator for your login email and password. You can also 👆 click "Register here" to create a new account with your business name.

📝 How to Register (New Account)

1

👆 Click "Register here" on the login page

This takes you to the registration form.

2

Fill in Your Information

Enter your Full Name, Company/Shop Name, Email, and Password (minimum 6 characters).

3

👆 Click Register Now

Your account will be created. You will be redirected to the login page.

📦 3. Products & Categories

Manage all your stationery products and organize them into categories.

🏷️ Categories

Categories help you group similar products together (e.g., "Pens", "Paper", "Notebooks").

1

Go to 🏷️ Categories from the sidebar menu

You will see a list of all your categories.

2

👆 Click ➕ Add Category

A form will appear. Enter the Name and Description. Then 👆 click 💾 Save.

✏️ Edit — Change category details   

📦 Products (Items)

This is where you manage all your products/items in the system.

1

Go to 📦 Products from the sidebar menu

You will see a table of all products with their details.

2

👆 Click ➕ Add Product to add a new item

Fill in the product details form (see the fields below).

📷 Barcode Scanning

📷

Scan with Camera

👆 Click the 📷 Scan button to open your camera. Point at a barcode — the system will read it automatically.

🔌

Scan with USB Scanner

Simply scan the barcode with your USB scanner. The system automatically detects the barcode and finds the matching product.

💡
Bulk Import: 👆 Click the CSV Import button to upload many products at once from a spreadsheet file.

🖨️ Print Barcodes

1

👆 Click the 🖨️ printer icon on any product row

A dialog will ask how many labels to print (default: 20).

2

👆 Click Print — barcode labels will be generated in A4 format

A print window will open. Select your printer and print.

ℹ️
Product Actions: 👁️ View ✏️ Edit 🔧 Adjust Stock 🗑️ Delete (Admin only)

📋 Purchase Requisitions

A requisition is a request to buy items. It must be approved before becoming an order.

1

Go to 📋 Requisitions from the sidebar

You will see all existing purchase requests.

2

👆 Click ➕ New Requisition

Select the Store, add Purpose/Notes, then add items.

3

Add Items to Your Request

Select products from the dropdown, set quantities. You can also 📷 scan barcodes to add items quickly. 👆 Click ➕ Add Line for more items.

4

👆 Click Submit Requisition

Your request will be sent for approval.

✅ Approving Requisitions

🔒
Only Admin and Manager can approve or reject requisitions.
✅ Approve ❌ Reject — choose one for each pending request

📜 Requisition Status Guide

StatusMeaning
📝 DraftNot yet submitted
⏳ PendingWaiting for approval
✅ ApprovedApproved — ready to order
❌ RejectedNot approved (with reason)

🛒 Purchase Orders

Orders are sent to suppliers to buy products.

1

Go to 🛒 Orders from the sidebar

View all purchase orders and sales in one place.

2

👆 Click ➕ Create New Order

Select the Supplier, set Expected Delivery Date, and add items with quantities and unit prices.

3

➕ Add Items to the Order

For each item, select the product, enter quantity and unit price. The total is calculated automatically. 👆 Click ➕ Add Item for more lines.

4

👆 Click Submit

Then use 📧 Send to send it to the supplier.

📜 Order Actions

👁️ View Details 📧 Send 📥 Receive 🔒 Close

📥 Goods Receiving

When goods arrive from a supplier, record what you received here.

1

Go to 📥 Goods Receiving from the sidebar

You will see a history of all received goods.

2

👆 Click 📥 Log Received Goods

Select the Purchase Order from the dropdown (only undelivered orders appear).

3

Enter Received Quantities

For each item in the order, enter how many you actually received. You can receive partially — the system tracks this.

4

👆 Click Fulfill & Receive Stock

Stock levels will be automatically updated! ✅

📤 6. Distribution & Item Issues

Track when items are issued to departments, stores, or individuals.

📤 Item Issues (to Stores)

Issue items from your main inventory to specific stores.

1

Go to 📤 Item Issues from the sidebar

View all past issue records.

2

👆 Click 📤 Issue Items

Select the Destination Store from the dropdown.

3

Add Items

Search and select products (shows available stock), enter quantities. Add issuance notes. 👆 Click ➕ Add Item for more.

4

👆 Click Issue Items

Items will be transferred and stock updated.

📋 Distribution (to Departments/People)

Issue items to specific users and departments within your organization.

1

👆 Click 📋 Issue Items

Select the user receiving the items, enter the department, and add any notes.

2

Add Line Items

Select items and quantities. 👆 Click ➕ Line to add more items.

3

👆 Click Issue

The distribution is recorded and stock is deducted.

⚠️
Distribution is only available for: Admin Manager Inventory Clerk

🧾 8. Sales (Point of Sale)

The Sales page is your cash register. Create sales, scan items, and process payments.

💰

Today's Revenue

Total earnings for today.

🧾

Transaction Count

Number of sales today.

🛒 How to Make a Sale

1

Go to 🧾 Sales from the sidebar

The sale creation form appears at the top.

2

Add Items to the Sale

You have 3 ways to add items:

📷 Camera Scan — click the scan button
🔌 USB Scanner — just scan, auto-detects
🔍 Search — type item name to find and select

3

Set Quantities and Review

Adjust quantities for each item. You can also add Services (toggle between Item/Service for each line).

4

Enter Payment Details

Enter Customer Name, select Payment Method (Cash, etc.), and enter Amount Paid.

5

👆 Click Complete Sale

The sale is recorded and stock is automatically deducted. ✅

💡
Unpaid Sales: If a customer pays partially, the sale appears in the "Unpaid Sales" section below. 👆 Click Pay Balance to record additional payments later.
💡
Scanning tip: If you scan a barcode for an item already in the cart, the system automatically increases the quantity instead of adding a duplicate.

👤 12. Users & Roles

RoleWhat They Can Do

💡
Block vs Delete: Blocking a user prevents them from logging in but keeps their data. Deleting removes them permanently.

🔧 15. Troubleshooting

Common issues and their solutions.

❓ Problem✅ Solution
Can't log in Check your email and password. Make sure Caps Lock is off. Ask Admin to check if your account is blocked.
Can't see a menu item Your role may not have access. Ask your Admin to enable the module for your role in Settings.
Barcode scanner not working 📷 Camera: Allow browser to access camera when prompted. Try Chrome browser.
🔌 USB: Make sure scanner is plugged in. Click inside any input field first, then scan.
Can't add/edit items You may not have the right role. Only Admin, Manager, and Inventory Clerk can modify products.
Stock numbers seem wrong Check the Stock Movement History tab to see all changes. An Admin can make a stock adjustment to correct quantities.
PDF/Invoice not downloading Disable pop-up blockers in your browser. Try a different browser (Chrome recommended).
Page loading slowly Check your internet connection. 👆 Click the 🔄 Refresh button. Try clearing your browser cache.
Forgot password Ask your Admin to reset your password via the Users page (🔑 Change Password action).
💡
Need more help? Contact your system administrator or the dealer who set up your system.
